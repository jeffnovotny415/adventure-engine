## Naming notes

- Captain Aster may be called Aster; Captain Thyme may be called Thyme.
- Kent Trailway may be called Kent.
- The Stasis uses “the Stasis” naturally within sentences.
- The Fancy Spoon is also referred to as “the Spoon.”
- Asher Portable is the portable form of Asher, not a separate character.
- Grabs Awesome Structures spells **GAS**. Grabs Grand Plaza is the planned development.
- **Frost station** and **Project Fix em** retain the requested capitalization and wording.
- Reader-chosen placeholders stay intact in the story source.
