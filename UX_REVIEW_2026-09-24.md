# Reader UX review — September 24, 2026

## Changes

- **Stable reading toolbar.** The old “Hide controls” text link wrapped beneath the navigation once “Back to choice” appeared. At 390×844 this increased the toolbar from 66px to 110px and pushed page navigation below the screen. The visibility control is now a labeled, 44px eye button in a single row. Bookshelf, saved passages, and reading settings retain their accessible names. Toolbar text can grow within a compact range; story text retains its full sizing range.
- **First-page choice undo.** Per Jeff's direction, “Back to choice” replaces Previous in the lower-left navigation slot on the first page of a scene with choice history. Subsequent pages show Previous. The choices screen itself has no undo button; returning to the passage and its first page permits another undo. Continuous VoiceOver reading retains an undo action in its passage footer.
- **Usable illustration viewer with large system text.** On 568×320 at the largest simulated system text category, viewer controls previously consumed the whole canvas (0px image height). Bounded toolbar/help text now leaves room for the illustration while retaining zoom, fit, close, and rotation.
- **Large-text headings.** The bookshelf heading overflowed a 390px phone by 3px, and the name-entry heading overflowed to 456px and was clipped by the paper edge. Headings and setup labels can now break long words without reducing text size.

The leather books, parchment treatment, illustrations, story wording, story choices, and save format are unchanged.
