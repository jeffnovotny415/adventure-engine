## scene_002 — Rush Outside

You rush to open the garage door, hearing something scraping around outside your home.

“Dang it! First thing next time.”

Not ideal, but you decide to try your luck with a few well-placed throws.

Direct hit! The red eye light slowly blinks as you throw the second rock, which knocks the robot right over, and the eye light goes out.

You approach slowly, making sure it will not reboot.

Looks like you got it! A fully assembled robot this close to home? JACKPOT!

“Asher, how long until the diagnostic is done?”

“Got it,” you say, looking down at your handy wrench. “Then I guess it’s finally time to upgrade this thing.”
