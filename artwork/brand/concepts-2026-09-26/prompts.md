# Paths of Wonder logo explorations

Review concepts generated with the built-in Imagegen tool. These are not approved production logos. Existing site branding remains unchanged.

## Choose a door

File: `01-choose-a-door.png`

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE original polished logo concept for "Paths of Wonder", an interactive adventure reading app for children and families. User wants the logo to express choice, adventure, fun, silliness and story, and to belong to a lovingly worn illustrated book. The existing conventional book-and-star serif logo felt bland. Exact text, all clearly legible: "Paths of Wonder". Genuine transparent alpha background. Wide 3:2 composition, comfortably contained with breathing room, suitable for comparing brand concepts. Brand palette dark walnut ink #33291f, warm ivory paper #eee5d4, muted brass #987749, small oxblood #713d32 and dusty teal accents. Professional expressive pen and ink with restrained colored-pencil character, strong logo silhouette and confident shapes, NOT a full scene illustration. Bespoke warm readable lettering, purposeful character and slightly irregular bookish charm. NO Harry-Potter-like wordmark, lightning serifs, generic corporate typography, glossy gradients, mockup, checkerboard, tagline, concept label, watermark, background panel, people or hero characters. Emblem and typography must have generous intentional spacing. Keep word spaces clear. This is a logo, no poster or whole book cover. Concept: a single open, gently battered storybook whose three upright pages fold into THREE different doorways, one left, one forward, one right, visibly offering the reader a choice. Doors are slightly crooked and playful, with a star-shaped cutout in one, a tiny gear-shaped knob in another, and a leaf-shaped hinge in the third; these are integrated details, not floating extra icons. A small warm gold path forks across the open pages toward the doors. Delightful impossible paper architecture and a sense of invitation; humorous rather than solemn. Below the emblem, a compact two-line hand-lettered rounded slab-serif title, "Paths of" above "Wonder", joyful balanced proportions. Visually memorable paper doorway silhouette. Avoid wizard-school heraldry and tiny clutter.
