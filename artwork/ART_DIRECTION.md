# Paths of Wonder illustration rules

These rules record Jeff's artwork direction from September 23, 2026.

## Reader's hero

- Show the reader's hero from the side or behind, with the face turned away and fully concealed. POV details are also welcome.
- Do not reveal skin color, hair color, facial features, or gender through the illustration. Cover hands with neutral charcoal work gloves, wrists with sleeves, neck/head with a raised hood, and legs with trousers and boots.
- Avoid exposed fingertips, ears, neck, hair, or face profiles, even in small background views.
- Supporting named characters and neighborhood bystanders can have visible faces; these identity constraints apply to the reader's hero.

## Medium and color

- Preserve the established professional ink, graphite crosshatching, and colored-pencil style. Use the approved character artwork for shape continuity.
- Narrative drawings can be fully colored, including robots and equipment. The Can Opener blueprint's pale paper treatment is a diagram convention, not the physical tool's color.
- The real Can Opener has steel/gunmetal surfaces, copper coils, and a blue power core. Its active energy axe blade is blue electrical energy.
- Tool mode: the axe blade is completely absent, not replaced by a white or steel blade. Switch it off when repairing, climbing, resting, carrying, or setting the tool down. It is normally active only in robot combat; the scripted finale discharge is a separate illustrated action.
- A tool on a workbench rests flat against the tabletop. It must not hover or balance upright without physical support.
- The Wrist Wrock-It is a cylindrical spring/coil rock launcher. Do not substitute the wrench in its experiment scenes.
- The moldy-sandwich drawing includes the sound effect `PLOP` beside the sandwich, per Jeff's request.

## Book use

- Mix larger scene/opening illustrations with smaller comic moments between passages. This is a pacing reference, not a request to copy another book's drawing style.
- Keep illustrations after the prose that reveals their contents; opening images may reuse things already introduced.
- Never change authored prose to accommodate art. Store image placement and accessibility descriptions separately.
- Keep actual alpha transparency for drawings that sit on parchment; no baked checkerboard or opaque dark backdrop.
- Generated alternatives remain review drafts until accepted. Keep existing app art intact unless replacement is authorized.
