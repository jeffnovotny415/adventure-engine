# Seven, the synthoid

New character concept requested by Jeff, using approved Captain Azul and Pip
artwork as style and clothing references. This is not a cleanup of a supplied
sketch. Built-in image generation; exact prompt and references in prompts.json.

Grounded in Space Walker scenes 006 and 008: fully metal, fluid movement, scanning
eyes, loose grey jumpsuit with a central zipper and many patches. His strength
and precise fingers inform the articulated hands. Depicted before later damage.

Pewter finish, amber lens eyes and face-plate design are visual interpretations,
not additional authored facts. No story text changed. Chapter placement remains
unassigned; the asset is outside the shipped app bundle.
