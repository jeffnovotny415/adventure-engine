# Space Walker crew outfits

Only drawings 7 and 8 are revised in this pass:

- Drawing 7 is Pip, the pilot with wild yellow hair and antennae-eyes.
- Drawing 8 is Captain Azul, the tall blue captain with wide ears.

The identity mapping comes from `scene_006`, `scene_007` and `scene_008` in the
live `webapp/src/data/stories/space_walker.json`. Scene 006 describes the entire
bridge crew in "loose-fitting grey jumpsuits with zippers down the middle and
patches everywhere." Scene 029 repeats the middle zipper and many patches.

Both characters therefore wear the same practical one-piece grey crew outfit,
adapted to their different builds. Specific patch emblems are not described in
the text, so the illustrations use simple fabric patches without invented rank
insignia, slogans or logos. The approved faces, colors, poses and rendering style
are retained. Other illustrations are unchanged.

Created with built-in image editing; the exact prompts and source references are
recorded in `prompts.json`. Revision 2 and the original photographs remain intact.
The main gallery and its detail view display these updated outfits. No authored
story content is modified, and chapter placement remains unassigned.
