# Pip face and character refinement

Reviewed Pip against Space Walker scenes 006, 008, 016 and 027. Her short build,
wild yellow hair, two antennae-eyes and patched grey zipped jumpsuit were already
present. Refined the rigid tooth-grid grin into a closed expressive smile, added
individual hair strands and wisps, and differentiated her pupil directions to
reflect her ability to watch two things at once. No invented equipment or insignia.
