## Interpretation for review

- Ship perspective and mechanical detail are refined from the silhouettes and annotations.
- Genesis keeps its orange passenger hull and docking clamp; Stasis has no windows and a damaged rear cargo opening.
- The Rescue Corps ship has an observation deck, front emitter dish, and shuttle bay; its name remains reader-defined.
- The three-cell sketch is combined with the text's blue glass engine column.
- Zenta follows the note describing an orange relative of Percy, with an apron and fried dough from the character description.
- The drone retains four propulsion units and cameras above and below, with Grabs's ownership message.
- Asher uses a computer tower and HUD monitor, without a humanoid face.
- Elliana keeps purple hair, yellow slit-pupil eyes, cat ears, and a fluffy tail. Clothing is an artistic interpretation of the sketch.
- Syphon remains faceless, combining moth wings and a jellyfish body.
- Honey keeps a round furry body, large dark eyes, small wings, and a long fluffy tail.
